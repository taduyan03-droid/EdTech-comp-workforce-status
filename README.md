# Workforce Status Analysis – Topica English

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the workforce structure and employee turnover
situation at Topica English.

The analysis focuses on employee distribution across locations, branches,
levels, departments, seniority, employment status, and reasons for leaving.

The objective is to understand the company's workforce structure,
identify potential organizational issues, and evaluate employee retention,
particularly within the sales function.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze the overall workforce structure
- Understand employee distribution across locations and branches
- Analyze employees by level, department and seniority
- Identify the company's main workforce functions
- Analyze employee turnover and employment status
- Identify the main reasons for employee departures
- Evaluate employee retention issues within the sales function

## Analysis

### 1. Overall Workforce Distribution

The analysis shows that approximately 72% of employees are based in Hanoi,
reflecting the location of the company's headquarters.

The workforce is distributed across multiple branches and locations,
including Ho Chi Minh City and other Southeast Asian locations.

However, employee distribution is not evenly balanced across locations
and branches, which may contribute to differences in operational
effectiveness and business performance.

## 2. Workforce by Level and Department

The workforce structure indicates that the company is operating with a
strong sales-oriented growth model.

A large proportion of employees are concentrated in:

- Enrollment Consultants
- Specialist-level positions
- Other specialist roles

This indicates that sales and customer acquisition are major priorities
for the company.

The analysis suggests that the company is prioritizing revenue growth
rather than investing equally in areas such as infrastructure, software,
or educational program development.

## 3. Workforce by Seniority

Employee tenure analysis shows a strong concentration of employees with
0–1 year of service.

The Enrollment Consultant and Specialist groups account for a large
proportion of recently recruited employees.

This reinforces the observation that the company has been actively
expanding its sales workforce in recent years.

At the same time, the "Other Specialists" group is distributed across
different levels of tenure, suggesting that this group represents a broad
range of specialized roles within the organization.

## 4. Employee Turnover

The analysis identifies a high level of employee turnover.

More than 1,000 employees are recorded as having terminated their
employment, representing approximately 50% of the total workforce.

Most departures are initiated by employees themselves, while departures
through mutual agreement or company-initiated termination account for a
relatively small proportion.

This suggests that employee retention is a significant organizational
challenge.

## 5. Turnover by Position

Specialists account for more than 67% of employees who left the company,
followed by full-time collaborators at approximately 14.37%.

Enrollment Consultants are also among the groups with the highest number
of departures.

Because these positions are closely related to the company's sales
function, high turnover in these groups may create significant operational
and revenue risks.

## 6. Potential Causes of Turnover

The analysis indicates that employee-initiated resignation is the main
reason for turnover.

Several possible factors are discussed, including:

- High performance targets and KPI pressure
- Compensation and benefits
- Job competitiveness and pressure
- Employee selection and recruitment quality

However, the dataset does not contain information about employee income
or recruitment standards, so these factors cannot be confirmed as direct
causes of turnover.

## Key Findings

- Approximately 72% of employees are located in Hanoi.
- Employee distribution across branches and locations is uneven.
- Sales-related positions represent the company's most important
  workforce group.
- A large proportion of employees have worked for the company for
  0–1 year.
- More than 1,000 employees have terminated their employment,
  representing approximately 50% of the workforce.
- More than 67% of employees who left were Specialists.
- Enrollment Consultants are also one of the groups with the highest
  turnover.
- Employee-initiated resignation is the dominant reason for departure.
- Employee retention, particularly within the sales function, is a major
  organizational issue.

## Business Implications

The company's strong reliance on sales personnel makes workforce stability
particularly important.

High turnover within sales-related positions may increase recruitment and
training costs, disrupt operations, and potentially affect revenue
generation.

The analysis therefore suggests that improving employee retention in the
sales function should be a key organizational priority.

Further investigation into compensation, KPI structures, recruitment
quality, employee satisfaction, and working conditions would help identify
the underlying causes of turnover more precisely.

## Conclusion

The workforce analysis indicates that Topica English was in a period of
development and expansion, with significant resources concentrated in
sales-related functions.

However, the company also faced substantial employee turnover, especially
among Specialists and Enrollment Consultants.

The combination of rapid recruitment and high turnover may create
challenges for maintaining workforce stability and sustaining business
growth.

Employee retention within the sales function therefore represents one of
the most important issues identified in this analysis.
